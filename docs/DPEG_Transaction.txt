    01 OVERVIEW & OBJECTIVES

Background

The Acquisitions & Dispositions (A&D) department at DPEG manages the full lifecycle of commercial real estate
transactions -- from contract execution through post-closing handoff to property management. At any given time, the
A&D team is running 10 to 15 simultaneous deals, each with its own critical dates, vendor coordination, financing
structure, and legal requirements.

Currently, deal tracking is managed via a manual Excel critical dates sheet, physical Manila folders, calendar
reminders, and email. This creates significant risk of tasks falling through the cracks -- particularly on parallel
workstreams that are not dependent on each other but must all be completed within a tight feasibility window.

Primary Objectives for Salesforce

             · Central deal record for every transaction -- single source of truth replacing Excel tracking sheet

             · Auto-generate ALL tasks simultaneously the moment a deal is created (tasks are parallel, not
             sequential)

             · Task due dates dynamically tied to critical dates (earnest money due, feasibility end, closing date)

             · Conditional task groups: financing tasks activate only when 'Loan Required' = Yes on the deal
             record

             · Dashboard visibility: overdue tasks and upcoming deadlines across all 10­15 active deals at a
             glance

             · Vendor tracking: BBG, LPRD, appraisers, insurance brokers, title company -- deliverable status
             per deal

             · Post-closing checklist automation: utilities, loan setup, document uploads, tenant notifications
             · Deal stage progression: Contract Executed  Due Diligence  Pre-Closing  Closed  Asset
             Under Management

Key Design Principle

         s ALL tasks spawn simultaneously on Day 1 of the deal. Nothing waits for anything else. The
         team operates at maximum velocity from the moment the contract is executed. Task urgency is
         driven by the feasibility window -- a 5-day feasibility is a sprint; a 60-day deal (30-day feasibility
         + 30-day close) is the standard marathon baseline.

    05 AUTOMATION & FLOW REQUIREMENTS

Flow 1 -- Deal Creation Task Blast

             · Trigger: Deal record created with Stage = Contract Executed
             · Action: Create ALL tasks in Groups A through H2 simultaneously
             · Due dates calculated dynamically from: Contract Executed Date, Earnest Money Due Date,
             Feasibility End Date, Closing Date
             · Condition: Tasks in Group F (Financing) only create if Loan Required = Yes
             · Assign tasks to correct owner based on task type (Danish, Accounting, Legal, FES, Property
             Mgmt.)

Flow 2 -- Post-Closing Task Blast

             · Trigger: Deal Stage changes to Closed
             · Action: Create ALL tasks in Group J simultaneously
             · Escalation: If any Group J task is not completed within 3 business days, send alert to Danish
             · Auto loan payment task (Group J) should have a separate high-priority flag -- this is frequently
             missed

Flow 3 -- Critical Date Alerts

             · Earnest Money Due Date - 2 days: Alert Danish to request wiring instructions
             · Feasibility End Date - 5 days: Alert if any open due diligence tasks remain (PCR, LPRD, appraisal,
             estoppels)
             · Feasibility End Date - 2 days: Red flag alert -- any open feasibility tasks
             · Closing Date - 7 days: Alert to request closing statement
             · Closing Date - 2 days: Alert to verify wiring instructions
             · Insurance Binding Deadline - 3 days: Alert if insurance not yet bound

Flow 4 -- Loan Required Conditional Logic

             · On deal record creation: if Loan Required = No, suppress all Group F financing tasks
             · If Loan Required is changed to Yes after record creation: trigger Group F task creation retroactively
             · Consider a prompt at deal creation: 'Is this deal being financed?'  Yes / No dropdown

Dashboard Requirements

             · Active Deals Board: All deals in stages 1­5, with feasibility end date and days remaining
             · Overdue Tasks View: All tasks past due date across all active deals, sorted by urgency

    06 WIRE FRAUD PREVENTION PROTOCOL

         ss CRITICAL COMPLIANCE REQUIREMENT: Every wire transfer -- both earnest money and
         closing funds -- must follow this verification protocol without exception. Salesforce task
         checklists should enforce this as a required step before marking any wire task complete.

    Step 1: Request wiring instructions from title company or lender via email
    Step 2: CALL the title company / lender directly (do not use the phone number from the email -- look it up
    independently) to verbally verify every digit of the wiring instructions
    Step 3: Confirm: Bank name, ABA routing number, account number, account name, reference number
    Step 4: Provide verified wiring instructions to accounting team -- both the soft copy from email AND a written
    confirmation of the verbal verification
    Step 5: Accounting sends wire only after receiving BOTH the document and verbal confirmation note
    Step 6: Log verification in Salesforce task notes: 'Verified by phone with [name] at [phone number] on [date/time]'

Salesforce Implementation Note: The wire verification task should have a mandatory checkbox field 'Verbal
Verification Completed' that must be checked before the task can be closed. Consider adding a required text field for
the name of the person who confirmed and the phone number used.

    08 IMPLEMENTATION NOTES & KNOWN PAIN POINTS

Critical Items That Have Historically Been Missed

         s The following are known failure points in the current manual process. Salesforce must treat
         these as high-priority flagged tasks with escalation alerts.

Auto Loan Payment Setup
Accounting team frequently forgets to set up auto loan payment post-closing. This task must be flagged as CRITICAL
in Salesforce with a mandatory completion checkbox and 3-day escalation alert.

Loan Package Distribution
Danish has experienced sending a signed term sheet without first distributing the loan package to all bankers.
Salesforce should enforce that 'Loan Package Sent' task is completed before 'Term Sheet Signed' task can be
closed.

Wire Fraud Verification
Every wire must include verbal verification. Salesforce should not allow wire tasks to close without a mandatory
'Verbal Verification Completed' checkbox.

Insurance Binding Deadline
Insurance binding deadlines are easily missed. Salesforce should send alerts 7 days and 3 days before binding
deadline.

Post-Closing Document Uploads
Survey, insurance docs, rent roll, closing statement, and loan info uploads often happen inconsistently. Salesforce
Group J tasks enforce this systematically.

Banker Outreach Coverage
Danish acknowledges he often only contacts 1­2 bankers rather than the full bankers list. Salesforce should prompt
distribution to full list with a 'Bankers Contacted' counter field.

30/60/90 Day Post-Close Check-In
Property management progress verification after closing is often deprioritized. Salesforce should auto-create
recurring check-in tasks at 30, 60, and 90 days post-close.

Email Integration Note

All third-party coordination (BBG, LPRD, title company, bankers, appraisers, insurance brokers) is managed via email
and will continue to be managed via email. Danish is not looking to replace email. However, Salesforce should allow
him to manually log email milestones against vendor tasks (e.g., mark 'BBG bid received' as complete, log date
received). If Salesforce can integrate with Gmail/Outlook to auto-log emails from known vendor contacts against a
deal record, this would be an ideal enhancement to explore in Phase 2.

Future Phase -- Cash Deal Workflow Variant

Once the full marathon deal workflow is built and operational, a simplified cash deal variant should be configured. In a
cash deal: Group F (financing tasks) are suppressed. The bank account for the deal entity is still required (for equity
capital deposits). The property condition report, appraisal, insurance, LPRD, and all due diligence tasks remain.
Feasibility windows for cash deals may be shorter (5­15 days), so task urgency logic should adapt accordingly.
